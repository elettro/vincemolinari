To Do

Roadmap to 90+ SEO & AIO

1

Technical SEO Infrastructure

Create sitemap.xml and robots.txt, normalize canonical tags, strengthen page metadata, and remove public placeholder/internal text.

2

Schema Foundation

Add WebSite, WebPage, and standalone FINTECH.TV Organization schema while preserving existing Person and BreadcrumbList schema.

3

AIO Direct Answer FAQ

Add clear Q&A content that directly answers who Vince is, what FINTECH.TV is, speaking topics, booking, videos, and media assets.

4

Video SEO / VideoObject Schema

Keep the Google Sheet video feed working while adding static crawlable video context and structured data for known videos.

5

Media Kit Image SEO

Improve image alt text, asset labels, category descriptions, and ImageObject schema for key press assets.

6

Final Domain Swap

Once the domain is pointed, update all canonicals, sitemap URLs, robots, OG URLs, and schema IDs to the final public domain.

7

Search Console & Indexing

After launch, submit the sitemap, inspect URLs, request indexing, and monitor impressions, clicks, and query data.
