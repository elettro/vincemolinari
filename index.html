Who is Vince Molinari?

Vince Molinari is a Wall Street veteran, founder and CEO of FINTECH.TV, and global finance speaker focused on capital markets, digital assets, sustainable finance, fintech innovation, and financial media.

FINTECH.TV is a financial media platform covering capital markets, digital assets, fintech innovation, sustainable finance, and global markets through interviews, market conversations, and live financial media programming.

Vince Molinari speaks about capital markets, digital assets, fintech innovation, sustainable finance, tokenization, financial media, market access, regulation, and the future of global finance.
